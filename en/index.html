What is hydroseeding?

We spray a slurry of grass seed, wood-fibre mulch, starter fertiliser, tackifier and water onto prepared soil. The mulch keeps the seed moist and protected, the tackifier holds it in place, even on steep slopes and in heavy rain. Grass usually comes up within 7 to 15 days.

How it works

  1. Site visit: measurement, soil, access for the trailer, water supply.
  2. Firm quote within 48 hours, line by line.
  3. Light groundwork to prepare the soil, then Gardena smart irrigation if you want it.
  4. Hydroseeding in half a day for a typical garden.
  5. Germination guarantee: at day 45, at least 80% of the area must be green, or we reseed the bare patches (with the watering schedule followed).
